Accuracy
Accuracy 100%. All three crabs have defence roll 640.

All three spots share one clock. Sand and ammonite crabs only pay for the hitpoints they have left. Red numbers are the rest of the hit. The gemstone crab pays for the whole hit, at 87.5%. The graph is that same style XP.

Sand crabs are 60 hitpoints. Ammonite crabs are 100 hitpoints and pay full XP. Both respawn after 30 seconds. Earth spells' 25% ammonite weakness is not included. The gemstone crab does not die. Space pauses. Figures follow the Old School RuneScape wiki.
